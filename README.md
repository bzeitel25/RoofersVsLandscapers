**A chaotic, asymmetrical multiplayer hero shooter built in Godot 4.**

Welcome to the ultimate turf war. In *Roofers vs Landscapers*, two bitter rival construction and maintenance crews fight for total dominance over a suburban battlefield. 

Will you take the high ground and defend your roof, or will you command the yard and siege the house?

## 🎮 The Gameplay Loop

The core of the game is an asymmetrical siege fueled by attrition, advanced movement, and completely unique class synergies:

*   **The High Ground vs The Siege:** Roofers spawn on top of the house with a finite supply cache. Landscapers spawn in the yard, using ladders, vehicles, and gadgets to breach the roof. The Roofers must manage their limited ammo and outlast the relentless assault.
*   **Fluid Parkour & Traversal:** Movement is everything. Players can seamlessly vault up ledges, slide down angled roofs at breakneck speeds, deploy rideable ziplines, and utilize double/triple jumps depending on their class weight.
*   **Unique Weapon Synergies:** Weapons aren't just for doing damage—they interact with each other. A Roofer can pin a Landscaper with the *Nail Gun*, then use the *Pry Bar* alt-fire to violently rip the nails out for burst damage. Or shoot a *Hot Tar* blob to slow an enemy, and ignite them with a flare. 
*   **Vehicles & Destruction:** Hop onto "Big Tony's Rocket Mower" to run down enemies (which explodes and ejects you when destroyed), or use the Lumberjack's Felling Axe to instantly shatter enemy fortifications and gadgets.

## 🛠️ The Crews & Archetypes

Choose from a massive roster of specialized workers, broken down by movement archetypes:

**The Roofers (Defenders of the Shingles)**
*   *The Nailer (Medium):* The all-rounder. Uses the Nail Gun and Pry Bar for deadly close-quarters synergies.
*   *The Tar King (Heavy):* Area denial. Shoots slowing tar and ignites it to block off gutters and ladders.
*   *The Gutter Sniper (Medium):* Holds down long sightlines with a heavy bolt-action rifle (strict 5-shot limit).
*   *The Apprentice (Light):* Highly agile, double-jumping scout. 

**The Landscapers (Kings of the Lawn)**
*   *The Climber (Light):* The fastest class in the game, utilizing an exclusive Triple-Jump to scale the house in seconds.
*   *The Trimmer (Light):* Armed with a Weed Wacker, capable of revving the engine to dash across the yard and execute massive AoE strikes.
*   *The Lumberjack (Heavy):* A slow juggernaut with a Felling Axe that can instantly destroy any deployable gadget on the map in a single overhead swing.
*   *The Mower (Heavy):* Starts the game already mounted on a pilotable Rocket Mower vehicle.
